About Al-Fajar Islamic Academy

Learn the Quran. Live with its Light. — Upholding authentic Quranic scholarship and structured online education.

Our Institutional Identity

Al-Fajar Islamic Academy is a dedicated online Islamic educational institution designed to deliver structured Quranic learning to students of all ages. Named after "Al-Fajar" (The Dawn), our academy symbolizes the light of divine guidance illuminating hearts and homes at the start of every learner's spiritual journey.

We bridge the gap between traditional mosque-based Quranic discipline and modern online accessibility — offering organized courses, verified attendance, structured examinations, and caring mentorship under qualified instructors.

Our Mission

To make accurate Quran recitation with Tajweed, memorization (Hifz), and foundational Islamic knowledge accessible to every household through disciplined, compassionate, and well-structured online instruction.

Our Vision

To nurture a generation of confident, spiritually grounded Muslims who recite the Holy Quran accurately, understand its timeless guidance, and embody noble Islamic character in their daily lives.

Our Teaching Approach

1. Diagnostic Level Assessment

Every incoming student begins with a gentle recitation check to place them at the exact right starting point.

2. Phonetic Precision (Makharij & Sifaat)

Strong emphasis on correct Arabic articulation points from day one so students develop natural, effortless fluency.

3. Interactive Revision & Retention

Structured daily Sabaq (new lesson), Sabqi (recent revision), and Manzil (cumulative review) ensure lasting retention.

Our Educational Goals

  • ✓ Enable students to read the Holy Quran fluently without pronunciation errors.
  • ✓ Instill love, reverence, and practical understanding of Quranic teachings.
  • ✓ Equip students with essential daily Duas, proper Salah methodology, and Islamic etiquette.
  • ✓ Support dedicated learners in complete or partial Hifz-ul-Quran with disciplined revision plans.
